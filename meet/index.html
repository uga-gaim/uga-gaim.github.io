I share my calendar here with the hope that technologies can make our lives easier. Curious how this is done? Check out Cal.com.

You do not have to book from here. Email invitation is still the way I prefer. Simply find an available time period below and email your invitation to weiming@uga.edu with topics/agenda and your preferred meeting options, e.g., room # for in-person meetings (if my office, GGY 312) or Google/Zoom/Team links.

I will decline any booking request that comes straight from Cal.com if I do not recognize it. Please write to me first rather than booking a slot cold. If we have already been in touch, that is absolutely fine using Cal.com directly. Just note that only 30-minute meetings are allowed through Cal.com. If more time is needed, please email me the invitation instead.

If it is urgent and no available time can be found, please reach out to me directly so we can figure out something else.

The calendar updates itself after any scheduling changes. So you might see your booked slots turn busy right after you send me the invite. I will decline any meetings if I cannot make it but I will make sure to communicate this with you.
