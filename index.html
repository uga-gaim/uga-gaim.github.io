Decoding the planet through Geoinformatics and AI Modeling

At GAIM Lab we advance geospatial analytics and predictive modeling by combining Artificial Intelligence with Geoinformatics connecting models to the physical world through remote sensing and GIS to forecast extreme events and renewable energy production.

To integrate Geoinformatics and AI is not just to interpret the Earth as it is, but to anticipate how it will evolve in the future, both near and far.

Resilience against extreme weather with better forecasts: heatwaves and precipitation.

AI for energy market forecasting and renewable energy production optimization.

Sustainable accessibility in the Arctic with geospatial modeling.
