Geoinformatics and AI Modeling Lab @ UGA

Forecasts Beyond Accuracy

Accuracy tells you how a model performed. Uncertainty tells you how far to trust it.

As a research lab, we develop scalable, uncertainty aware algorithms to study and forecast extreme events, from atmospheric rivers and extreme precipitation to hail, heat waves, and severe wind. We care about their severity, their timing, their location, how they are changing, and the impact.

Our work joins AI modeling with geospatial datasets and physical models, and reports not only a prediction but the uncertainty it deserves. To integrate Geoinformatics and AI is not just to interpret the Earth as it is, but to anticipate how it will evolve in the future, both near and far.
